# 4-Minute Live Demo Script

1. **Frame it.** "This app gives a pre-publication forecast. It only sees what a creator controls before uploading, and it tells you how sure it is."
2. **Sidebar.** Pick *Music video* or *Tech review* under **Try an example** and press **Load example**. Mention the 16,307 unique India-trending videos and the R² figures.
3. **Forecast studio.** Press **Generate pre-publication forecast**. Point to the expected views with the **80 % interval** and say: "We measured that this interval held 79.7 % of the time on later uploads." Show the percentile card ("higher than X % of trending videos").
4. **Why this forecast.** Read the bar chart: "These are exact Shapley values. We reveal title, tags, description, timing and category in every order and average each group's effect; they add up to forecast minus a typical video." Note that category is usually the largest bar and is not editable.
5. **Best time.** Open the heatmap: "The same draft in all 168 weekly slots." Press **Use this slot** on the best one and say the swing is modest, so content matters more than timing.
6. **Title lab.** Show the ranked edits, press **Apply** on the top one, return to the studio and show the forecast move. Say: "These are associations the model learned, not guarantees."
7. **Similar videos.** "A completely different method: TF-IDF look-alikes. If both methods agree, confidence rises; if they differ, that is a warning."
8. **Scenario lab.** Change one thing in Draft B and compare. Point at the overlap message: "Most small edits fall inside the model's uncertainty, which is the honest answer."
9. **Model brief.** Show chronological-holdout R² and the coverage table.
10. **Close with the limit.** "The data only contains videos that already trended in India (2017-2018). It cannot decide whether an arbitrary upload will go viral."
